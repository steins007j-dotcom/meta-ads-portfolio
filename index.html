About me

Data-driven, creative-minded and accountable for results.

I'm a performance marketer based in Bengaluru, working at The Website Makers where I manage Meta Ads for clients like Roots Rebirth, Iris Flora, Svarnadeep, Chandana MUA, Sambrama Plate Decor and Waltractive.
